The Story

Experience Built Over a Lifetime

Ludo began his massage career in Slovakia, where he studied classical and sports massage and built the foundations of a career that has now spanned more than four decades.

After moving to Ireland in 2006, he continued his work in professional wellness and hospitality, including serving as a senior massage therapist at BrookLodge Hotel & Wells Spa in Macreddin Village.

In 2015, Ludo opened his own massage studio in Bray, Co. Wicklow, creating a dedicated space where clients could receive experienced, practical and individual treatment.

Since then, he has worked with thousands of clients, professional athletes, sports teams, corporate groups and people simply looking for expert massage from someone with genuine hands-on experience.
