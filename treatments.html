Sports Massage

For Active Bodies & Performance

Sports massage is designed for people who place regular physical demands on their bodies.

It can be used as part of a wider training or recovery routine and is suitable for runners, cyclists, rugby players, gym users and people involved in regular sport or physical activity.

Treatment is adapted depending on your activity, current condition and the areas that need the most attention.

Deep Tissue Massage

Focused Work for Muscular Tension

Deep tissue massage uses more focused pressure to work on areas of muscular tightness and tension.

It may suit clients who spend long periods sitting, work physically demanding jobs, train regularly or simply carry persistent tension in areas such as the back, shoulders, neck or legs.

The pressure is adjusted throughout the treatment and should always be appropriate for the individual.

Classical Massage

Traditional Massage for Relaxation & Wellbeing

Classical massage uses traditional techniques to create a more relaxing and balanced treatment.

It is ideal for clients who want to step away from daily stress, ease general muscular tension and give the body time to relax.

As with every treatment at Ludo Massage Studio, the session is adapted to the individual rather than following a rigid routine.

Your Appointment

What to Expect

The treatment starts with a short conversation so Ludo can understand what you need and tailor the session accordingly.

Step 01

Talk

Explain what brings you in, where you feel tension and what you would like to get from the treatment.

Step 02

Treat

The session is adapted to your body, comfort level, preferred pressure and the areas requiring focus.

Step 03

Recover

Give your body time after treatment and follow any practical guidance provided for the hours that follow.

Important

Massage therapy is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment. If you are dealing with an injury, ongoing pain or a medical condition, consult an appropriate healthcare professional.
