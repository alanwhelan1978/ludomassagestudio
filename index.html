Experience Matters

Over Four Decades of Hands-On Experience

Ludo studied Classical and Sports Massage in Slovakia before moving to Ireland in 2006.

He went on to work as a senior massage therapist at BrookLodge Hotel & Wells Spa in Macreddin Village, before opening Ludo Massage Studio in Bray in 2015.

Throughout his career, Ludo has delivered more than 14,000 massages and has worked with professional athletes, sports teams, corporate groups and clients from all walks of life.
